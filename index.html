Learn the correct order from images - no typing
Connect first, then give one or more VINs that are already in the correct order. The tool reads every image of those VINs (its angle/category as classified from the image), builds the order, and compares every scanned VIN's images against it.
Advanced: type / upload a label sequence manually (optional)
One label per line, top = first image. Labels: Exterior Front Right, Exterior (straight front), Exterior Front Left, Exterior Left, Exterior Rear Left, Exterior Rear, Exterior Rear Right, Exterior Right, Interior, Focus Shoot, Miscellaneous. Every exterior angle (e.g. 310, 40, 80, 130…) is grouped into the nearest of the 8 exterior positions. Labels you leave out go last in their current order; images with the same label keep their current relative order.
